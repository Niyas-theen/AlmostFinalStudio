# Almost Final: art direction and asset brief

Status: Claude cannot create photographic or photo-manipulated images. This brief is for a designer or an AI image tool. The hero background (`banner-hero.svg`) was approved. The seven category vector posters in `public/images/cat-*.svg` are temporary placeholders and should be replaced.

## 1. Shared visual system (so all seven feel like one campaign)
- **Photography:** real-looking people shot in black and white, high contrast, studio-lit, plain background, so each can be cut out cleanly. Mixed with colour photographic objects (real props), never illustrations.
- **Colour:** one pastel field per poster plus one big flat disc of a stronger tone from the same hue. Brand navy #0B263D appears only in small details.
- **Brand motif:** every poster carries a small element of the graded bars (navy #273674 to pale green #EDF6E8) so the set stays recognisably Almost Final: a ribbon, steps, a film strip pattern, a door frame, and so on.
- **Texture:** fine paper grain on the whole poster, torn or scissor-cut paper edges on photographic fragments, soft contact shadows under every cutout.
- **Scale play:** a very large object next to a small human figure creates the story.
- **Layout:** 4:3 for cards (1600×1200) and a 16:7 crop (2400×1050) for page banners. Keep the lower-left calm for a crop-safe margin; subjects sit in the right two-thirds.
- **Rules:** fictional people only, no logos, brands or readable text. Keep a record of the tool or licence for each asset. Never present an image as a real client or result.

### 1. What the website actually shows (measured in the browser)
Graphic Design is a **wide card**, so its image slot is a short, wide strip. The slot is filled with `object-fit: cover` and a centred crop, so the visible area changes with screen size:

| Screen width | Slot size (CSS px) | Ratio | What gets cropped from a 4:1 master |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1440 (desktop, max) | 800 × 204 | 3.92 : 1 | about 1% top and bottom |
| 1280 | 710 × 180 | 3.94 : 1 | about 1% top and bottom |
| 1100 (narrowest desktop) | 610 × 180 | 3.39 : 1 | about 7% from each side |
| 1099 (widest tablet) | 945 × 180 | 5.25 : 1 | about 12% from top and bottom |
| 820 (tablet) | 698 × 180 | 3.88 : 1 | about 1% top and bottom |
| 641 (smallest tablet) | 537 × 180 | 2.98 : 1 | about 13% from each side |
| 640 and below (phone) | 536 × 402 down to 298 × 223 | **4 : 3** | a 4:1 image would show only its middle third, so a separate phone image is needed |

The slot has 20 px rounded corners and no border once an image is present. Text sits **above** the slot, so nothing overlays the image.
