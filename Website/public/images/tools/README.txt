Logos used by the tools strip (src/components/ToolsStrip.astro).
Most are SVG reproductions of the official marks from the open-source Iconify "logos" and Simple Icons sets.
Final Cut Pro, Magnific and Adobe Firefly are NOT included: no accurate source was available.
To show them, save the official logo here as final-cut-pro.svg|png, magnific.svg|png, adobe-firefly.svg|png (square, transparent).
Follow each vendor's brand guidelines.
